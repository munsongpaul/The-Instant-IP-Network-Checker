Understanding Your Connection Data

Your Internet Protocol (IP) address is a unique numerical label assigned to your device when it connects to a network. It acts as an online return address, enabling websites and services to route data properly to your specific machine.

Your Internet Service Provider (ISP) is the organization or company that supplies you with access to the broader web. Knowing your current provider helps identify local service structures, unexpected data routing hops, or proxy configurations applied to your link.

Network speed assessments analyze how quickly file packets travel from content delivery infrastructure down to your browser canvas. Keeping track of bandwidth fluctuations helps spot network throttling or equipment issues before they interrupt heavy downloads or stream playbacks.
