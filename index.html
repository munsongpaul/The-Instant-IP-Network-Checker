Frequently Asked Questions

How does this tool find my IP address and ISP?

When you click the check button, our tool securely communicates with a public lookup system to read your network's public digital signature. It instantly reads your IP and your Internet Service Provider (ISP) without accessing your private personal files or browser history.

Is my data shared or stored?

No. Your privacy is critical. This network tool is 100% client-side, which means the calculations and network checks happen strictly within your internet browser. We do not host databases, track user history, or store network details on any server.

How accurate is the online internet speed test?

Our tool downloads a small, safe dummy file entirely inside your browser cache to calculate how many megabits per second (Mbps) your current connection can handle. For the most accurate results, ensure you aren't actively downloading large video files or streaming media in other browser tabs while running the check.
